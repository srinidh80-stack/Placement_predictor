SPLITTING CRITERIA COMPARISON (ENTROPY vs GINI vs MSE)
============================================================
Classification Criteria:
  - Entropy: H(S) = - sum(p_i * log2(p_i)). Information Gain = H(parent) - H(split)
  - Gini Impurity: Gini(S) = 1 - sum(p_i^2). Computationally faster (no logarithm)

Regression Criterion:
  - Mean Squared Error (MSE): MSE = (1/n) * sum((y_i - y_mean)^2)
