4. COMPARATIVE ARCHITECTURAL SYNTHESIS (SLIDE 18):
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Criterion              | Isolation Forest | One-Class SVM    | Autoencoder     
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Anomaly Signal         | Short path length | Outside boundary | High recon error
   Key Parameter          | contamination    | nu (bound)       | bottleneck & tau
   Large n Scaling        | Yes (near-linear) | Costly O(n^2-n^3) | Yes (mini-batch)
   Non-Linear Bound       | Yes (tree splits) | Yes (RBF kernel) | Yes (activations)
   Typical Domain         | Tabular, fraud   | Moderate tabular | Images, sequences
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. CONTRAST WITH LINEAR REGRESSION (SLIDES 9, 14, 17):
   - Linear regression is supervised and harmed by outliers (distorting beta coefficients).
   - Unsupervised anomaly detectors are built specifically to isolate and expose anomalies.
   - Contamination and nu explicitly govern false alarm bounds without modifying underlying data.
