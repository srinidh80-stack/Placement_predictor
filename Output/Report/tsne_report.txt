2. CONTROLLED INTER-CLUSTER DISTANCE EXPERIMENT (SLIDE 9):
   True High-D Gaps: Gap(1->2) = 31.79, Gap(2->3) = 284.57
   True High-D Separation Ratio: 8.95x
   Embedded 2D Gaps: Gap(1->2) = 28.86, Gap(2->3) = 29.1
   Embedded 2D Separation Ratio: 1.01x
   CONCLUSION: t-SNE objective minimizes KL-divergence strictly over local neighborhood
   probabilities. Consequently, global inter-cluster gaps compress towards ~1x.
   TAKEAWAY: NEVER interpret physical distance between separate clusters in a t-SNE plot.

4. PRACTICAL GUIDELINES & INTERPRETATION (SLIDE 15 & 20):
   - Perplexity controls effective neighborhood size (number of close neighbors).
   - Perp = 2: Overly fine local focus fragments true clusters into artificial micro-islands.
   - Perp = 30: Well-balanced representation separating high-aptitude placed cohorts.
   - Perp = 50+: Neighborhood becomes excessively broad, risking blending of distinct cohorts.
   - Note on New Points: t-SNE does not have a transform() function; adding new points
     requires re-fitting on the entire combined dataset.
