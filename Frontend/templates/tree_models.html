Decision Trees

Splitting criteria (Entropy vs. Gini), depth constraints, and pruning diagnostics.

1. Entropy / IG 2. Gini Impurity 3. Depth Pruning

💡 Decision Trees Overview

  • Entropy: Measures information disorder: $H(S) = -\sum p_i \log_2(p_i)$.
  • Gini Impurity: Measures probability of misclassification: $1 - \sum p_i^2$.
  • Pruning: Restricting max_depth prevents memorization of noise.

Bias-Variance Tradeoff

Diagnostic curves tracking underfitting (high bias) vs overfitting (high variance).

1. Underfitting 2. Overfitting Gap 3. Optimal Depth

💡 Bias-Variance Concepts

  • High Bias (Underfitting): Depth 1-2 trees miss complex relationships (low train & val accuracy).
  • High Variance (Overfitting): Deep trees memorize train data (100% train, declining val accuracy).
  • Optimal Tradeoff: The depth where validation error is minimized.

Random Forest & Bagging

Bootstrap aggregation, Out-of-Bag (OOB) validation, and decorrelated feature voting.

1. Bootstrap Bagging 2. OOB Evaluation 3. Feature Importance

💡 Random Forest Highlights

  • Bagging: Reduces variance by training trees on bootstrap samples with replacement.
  • Feature Subsampling: Considering only $\sqrt{p}$ features at each split decorrelates trees.
  • OOB Score: Evaluates ensemble on leftover unselected samples (~36.8%) without a test set.
