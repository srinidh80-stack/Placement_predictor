💡 K-Means Concepts

  • Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (Inertia): Measures compactness $\sum_{k=1}^K \sum_{x \in C_k} \|x - \mu_k\|^2$.
  • Silhouette Coefficient: Measures separation quality $\frac{b - a}{\max(a, b)} \in [-1, 1]$.
  • Zero-Leakage Clustering: Trained purely on scaled skills & academics with target withheld.

Hierarchical Clustering

Second group: Agglomerative bottom-up tree, 4 linkage rules, cophenetic correlation, and dendrograms.

1. Ward Linkage 2. Cophenetic Corr 3. Dendrogram Cuts

💡 Hierarchical Clustering Insights

  • Linkage Methods: Ward (variance-minimizing), Complete (max distance), Average (mean), Single (min distance).
  • Chaining Effect: Single linkage forms elongated chains; Ward produces balanced cohorts.
  • Adjusted Rand Index (ARI): Quantifies statistical agreement between K-Means and Ward tree cuts.
